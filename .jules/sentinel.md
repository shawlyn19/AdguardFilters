## 2025-05-14 - Transitive Dependency Overrides for ReDoS
**Vulnerability:** Several transitive dependencies (`picomatch`, `brace-expansion`, `smol-toml`, `yaml`) were flagged by `npm audit` for Regular Expression Denial of Service (ReDoS) and other vulnerabilities (GHSA-c2c7-rcm5-vvqj, GHSA-3v7f-55p6-f55p, GHSA-f886-m6hf-6m8v, GHSA-v3rj-xjv7-4jmq, GHSA-48c2-rrv3-qjmp).
**Learning:** Even when direct dependencies are up to date, their sub-dependencies can harbor vulnerabilities. In this project, `picomatch` required a split override: version `4.0.4` for most of the tree, but version `2.3.2` specifically under `micromatch` to maintain compatibility with legacy/stable tools like `@adguard/aglint`.
**Prevention:** Regularly run `npm audit` and use the `overrides` field in `package.json` to pin secure versions of transitive dependencies. Document the specific GHSA IDs in the `package.json` to provide context for these overrides. Always maintain CRLF line endings in `package.json` and `package-lock.json` using `unix2dos` after running `npm install`.
